# Comparison of OpenInvento with other tools


Choosing an inventory manager is not just about counting features.
It is about finding a tool that fits the way you organize your belongings,
equipment, and shared spaces.

OpenInvento is a self-hosted inventory manager built around flexible
categories, custom fields, hierarchical locations, and shared inventories.
Homebox focuses on household inventory, while InvenTree specializes in
parts, stock control, and manufacturing workflows.

## Why OpenInvento?

### Build an inventory that fits your needs

Not every inventory contains the same kind of information.

With OpenInvento, you can define custom fields for individual categories.
This lets you adapt your inventory to different kinds of items instead
of treating everything as the same generic object.

### Organize items around real-world locations

Use hierarchical locations to reflect how your belongings are actually
stored — from larger areas down to individual storage locations.

Combined with separate and shared inventories, this gives you a flexible
way to organize personal belongings, shared equipment, and different
collections.

### Find physical items without unnecessary friction

Generate QR codes and scan QR codes or barcodes to look up items.
OpenInvento also supports external barcode scanners, making it useful
for workflows where items are labeled and scanned rather than searched
for manually.

### Keep your inventory under your control

Run OpenInvento on your own infrastructure. Export your inventory to CSV,
use the REST API for integrations, and create backups of your data.

## Summary

OpenInvento stands out with category-specific custom fields,
hierarchical locations, separate and shared inventories, QR/barcode
support, and a self-hosted approach. Together, these features make it a
flexible choice for organizing many kinds of personal or shared inventory.

Homebox is the closest household-inventory comparison, while InvenTree
is the stronger fit for parts and stock management.
