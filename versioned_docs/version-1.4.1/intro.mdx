# Introduction to OpenInvento

<div style={{textAlign: 'center'}}>
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openinvento/openinvento/refs/heads/main/assets/banner_light.png" alt="OpenInvento Header" width="100%" />
</div>

**OpenInvento** is a modern, self-hosted inventory management system engineered for clarity, speed, and deep flexibility. Whether organizing personal collections, household items, lab supplies, workshop tools, or business assets, OpenInvento gives you full control over your physical inventory without sending your data to third-party clouds.

---

## Key Features

- **Intuitive Hierarchy**: Organize items naturally across **Inventories**, **Areas**, **Shelves**, and nested **Boxes** (containers/ bins).
- **Fast Drag & Drop**: Easily reorganize your physical space by dragging articles and containers between areas, shelves, and boxes.
- **Custom Fields & Categories**: Tailor fields per category (e.g., expiry dates for food/medicine, ISBNs for books, serial numbers for electronics) or globally across all items.
- **QR Code & Barcode Integration**: Generate printable label sheets (PDF) in A4, A5, and Letter formats, and perform instant lookups using scanners or mobile cameras.
- **Rich Media & Attachments**: Attach photos, invoices, receipts, datasheets, and manuals (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, etc.) directly to articles.
- **Team & Family Sharing**: Multi-user support with invitation controls, allowing multiple users to access and collaborate within shared inventories.
- **Stock Tracking & Alerts**: Track item quantities and receive low-stock notifications when replenishment is needed.
- **Docker-Ready**: Hassle-free deployment using Docker and Docker Compose with persistent data volumes.
- **REST API**: Full-featured API secured with API keys for custom integrations, automation scripts, and third-party tools.
- **Responsive & Modern UI**: Built with a clean interface featuring complete dark mode support and optimized mobile views.
