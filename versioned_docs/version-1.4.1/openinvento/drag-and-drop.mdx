# Drag and Drop

OpenInvento features an interactive drag-and-drop system that makes reorganizing physical storage fast, fluid, and intuitive.

---

## How to Use Drag and Drop

1. **Click and Hold**: Click and hold your mouse button (or press and hold on a touch screen) on any movable card or row.
2. **Drag**: Move the item across your screen. The interface provides visual feedback, lifting the item and highlighting valid drop targets.
3. **Drop**: Release the item over a compatible target zone (e.g., a Shelf header, another Box, or an Area container).

:::tip Visual Feedback
When dragging, valid drop targets highlight automatically with an accent border. Releasing an item outside a valid drop target will smoothly cancel the operation without modifying your data.
:::

---

## Supported Drag-and-Drop Actions

| Item Being Dragged | Valid Destinations | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article** | Another Shelf | Moves the article to that shelf |
| **Article** | An Area container | Moves the article directly to the root of that area |
| **Article** | A Box | Places the article inside the target box |
| **Box** | A Shelf | Stores the entire box (and all contained items) on the shelf |
| **Box** | An Area container | Places the box directly on the area floor |
| **Box** | Another Box | Nests the dragged box inside the target box as a sub-box |

---

## Nesting Containers

Because boxes can contain other boxes, drag-and-drop allows you to easily structure modular toolkits or sorting trays:

1. Drag a small box (e.g., *"Metric Bolts"*) over a larger container (e.g., *"Hardware Organizer"*).
2. Drop it onto the target box.
3. The small box becomes a child container. When you move or label the parent box, all child containers remain securely organized inside.

---

## Touch & Mobile Support

Drag-and-drop is fully optimized for mobile devices and tablets:
- Long-press on an item to initiate dragging without accidentally scrolling the page.
- Drop targets expand slightly on touch screens to make targeting effortless with fingers.
